Definitions. Orders and revenue count every order except canceled and unavailable ones; revenue is total payment value. Late % is late orders divided by orders with a delivery date. Review score is the mean of each order's latest review, over reviewed orders.

Summary cards. Totals cover all months. Averages are the plain mean of the monthly values and leave out the low-volume months (Sep 2016 and Sep 2018). November 2016 has no orders in the data.
