Definitions. Orders and revenue count every order except canceled and unavailable ones, and revenue is total payment value. Late % is late orders divided by orders with a delivery date. Review score is the mean over reviewed orders.

Summary cards. Totals cover the selected period. Averages are the plain mean of the monthly values and leave out Sep 2016 and Sep 2018, which have 1 to 2 orders each. The line under each average gives the figure weighted by orders. The delta compares with the period of equal length just before the selected one.

Low-volume months. Months with fewer than 30 orders are left off the rate charts unless the checkbox is on. They always stay in the table. Nov 2016 has no orders in the data.

Source: Olist marts (fct_orders). State is the customer's state.
